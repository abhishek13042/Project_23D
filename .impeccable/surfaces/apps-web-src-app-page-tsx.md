# The Survey

Mode: Experience on home and case, Operate on gallery and try, Read on results and about.

Research that shaped this: Segment Anything demo (one primary action, separate work and explanation modes), TotalSegmentator (the rules for an upload are stated before the drop zone), OHIF and the KiTS23 site were opened but gave no usable content.

Assigned world: topographic survey sheet. Assignment was run unattended, without a decision round.

Verdicts on challengers:
- Risograph web system: declined. Raise: commit to a limited ink set.
- Cutting bench select rail: declined. Raise: state is a mark, not a hue.
- Patent drawing sheet: competitive, loses because the terrain gives the site a data-born image. Raise: one annotation ink for what is under examination; every number says where it comes from.
- Op-art textile repeat: declined. Raise: the field regenerates at any width instead of being cropped.
- Bioluminescent plankton wake: declined. Raise: light only where something happened.
- Blacksmith scale shower: declined. Raise: nothing decorative survives the process.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Segmentation is outlining, and a contour line is an outline. The site is the survey sheet of 73 scans: every result is a measured height, and the failures stay on the map as pits.

OWN-WORLD: Survey sheet. Petrol chart ink ground, pale cool chart paper, a single-hue hypsometric ramp where higher scores are lighter, amber annotation ink reserved for what is under examination, a neat-line, margin ticks, a scale bar as the one ruler, a map key, a sheet index for the gallery, a title block as the footer. Never cream, never neon, never a hospital stock photo. Kidney, tumor and cyst keep their validated label colors. The viewer stays near-black, because film is read on black.

STORY: Arrive at the terrain, see at once that there are high plateaus and deep pits. Touch a spot and read one real scan. Open a scan and compare the outlines. Read how the numbers were made. Try your own, after the rules are stated.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Full-bleed petrol sheet. Title across the top in wide Archivo, the score terrain filling the rest: 73 real spot heights, smoothed contours inside a drawn limit of survey, the worst scans flagged in amber, a key and a live reading beside it. The first thing a visitor sees is the actual test result, not an illustration of it.

FORM: seed:d94852c551d19a7a. Code-led build, no raster assets, because no image tool exists here. Archivo with its width axis for sheet titles and UI, tabular numerals; Source Serif 4 italic for chart annotation only. Signature interaction: pointing at a spot height reads that scan's score and sizes; gallery spots open the scan. One authored motion: the contours survey in once on the home page, lowest first, off under reduced motion. Radius 2px. Marks carry state: triangle best, crossed ring worst, ring gallery scan, dot other scan.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
